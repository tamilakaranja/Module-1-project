YOUR FIRST JOURNEY

How to take a matatu

New to Nairobi? Follow these steps to make your first trip less stressful.

01

Find your stage

Ask for the correct boarding point for your destination.

02

Confirm the route

Check the destination sign and confirm with the conductor.

03

Check the fare

Confirm the current fare and available payment methods.

04

Watch your stop

Use familiar landmarks and ask when you are approaching.
