START HERE

Your job-search checklist

  • Prepare a clear, updated CV.
  • Identify the roles and industries you are interested in.
  • Use trusted sources to search for opportunities.
  • Prepare examples that demonstrate your skills.
  • Research an employer before an interview.
  • Read a job offer and contract carefully before agreeing.

YOUR FIRST JOB

Learn as you go.

You do not need to know everything on your first day. Ask questions, keep learning, communicate respectfully and give yourself time to adjust.
